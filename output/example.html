APPLICATIONS:

DNA and RNA

Enzymatic Synthesis

Controlled DNA and RNA enzymatic synthesis is an advanced technique that allows for the precise creation of sequences by using specific enzymes to add nucleotides under monitored conditions, ensuring control over the length and composition of the sequence.

One of the enzymes for enzymatic DNA synthesis is Terminal deoxynucleotidyl transferase (TdT). This enzyme has proven to be a reliable tool for synthesizing DNA without a template. TdT enzymes add nucleotides to the 3' end of a DNA molecule, enabling the construction of custom DNA/RNA driving advanced applications.

Commercially available TdT enzymes are not optimized for non-native nucleotide analogs required for the controlled synthesis. Blikka Genomics offers a diverse portfolio of six proprietary Terminal Deoxynucleotidyl Transferase (TdT) enzymes derived from artificial chimeric origins and designed for high coupling efficiency with both natural and modified nucleotides.
